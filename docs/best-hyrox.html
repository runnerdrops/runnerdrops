The 8 best shoes for HYROX.

Eight kilometers of running broken up by sleds, burpees and carries. The fastest shoe isn't the best shoe here; the one that survives the stations is. Ranked by our testing, the expert consensus, and 612 runners who've raced in them.

HOW

How this list is ranked

Weighted blend of the three tiers: 40% RunnerDrops testing (we raced in each pair), 30% expert consensus, 30% runner reviews filtered to people who mention HYROX, sled work or station racing. Shoes we haven't tested yet are ranked on the other two tiers and marked. Re-ranked every Monday as reviews come in.
